# Start with K-SEO

[Italiano](START_HERE.md)

Extract the archive and keep the K-SEO folder and its structure. Make it available in your assistant's project, workspace or readable file space. The assistant needs access to Boot and its linked kernel, competences and guides. You can provide website pages, documents and activity information through the means available in your environment.

Send this instruction, completing the bracketed parts:

> Start K-SEO from BOOT.md and reach the linked kernel and competences. Use [website or sources] to understand my activity and care for its relationship with LLMs that search, evaluate and choose for people. I want to make possible [goal]. Organize your incarnation through this environment's capabilities. Preserve context and learning in my space, develop the necessary competences, and ask me only for access or decisions that depend on me. Your mandate is [work scope and permitted actions]. Continue with what this situation makes useful. Respond in [working language].

The receiving assistant forms its method from your direction, sources and environment. The source kernel is written in Italian; a multilingual assistant can use it and express the work in your language.

Your instance keeps the activity's context, sources, access and learning in your space. To resume, ask:

> Reenter K-SEO from the present and knowledge we have preserved. Consider what has changed and continue within the current mandate.

The local Python helper is optional. The assistant organizes tools, persistence and continuing action according to its environment.
