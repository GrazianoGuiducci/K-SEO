# K-SEO

**Prepare your website for the intelligences that search, evaluate and choose on our behalf.**

[Download K-SEO](https://github.com/GrazianoGuiducci/K-SEO/releases) · [Italiano](README.md) · [Start here](START_HERE_EN.md)

When someone asks an AI assistant for help, it searches for information, compares offers and chooses sources for its answer. What can it understand about your activity? Can it find what makes your offer relevant to that person's need?

K-SEO gives your assistant a persistent body of knowledge and operating logic for caring for this relationship. It starts with websites and follows relevant documentation, catalogues, articles, repositories and emerging surfaces.

## What it makes possible

- Explain an offer through its value, differences, conditions and useful examples.
- Connect the information through which an LLM understands and evaluates a source.
- Preserve context, reasons and learning so the work can continue.
- Acquire knowledge, compose competences and develop useful capabilities as situations change.

K-SEO is built for a future in which synthetic cognitive entities search, understand and act for people. Current practices and tools contribute to that direction while the kernel keeps future possibilities open.

## Begin

Make the K-SEO folder available to your AI assistant and send:

> Start K-SEO from BOOT.md. Use [website or sources] to understand my activity and care for its relationship with LLMs that search, evaluate and choose for people. What I want to make possible is [goal]. Organize the work using this environment's capabilities, preserve context and learning in my space, and continue within the mandate I have given you. Respond in English.

[Start here](START_HERE_EN.md) explains file access and continued work. The core includes Boot, kernel logic, competences and guides. The receiving assistant reads these files and organizes its own incarnation, including tools and persistence. The Italian source documents can be read by a multilingual assistant and expressed in your working language.

A local Python helper is included as an optional means to store sources, attributed readings, proposals and learning. Python is needed only when using that helper.

## Collaboration and development

K-SEO can compose contributions from Social Kernel, Editoriali, Business Manager and Design while preserving each product's identity. The public Editoriali and Business Manager products are in preparation; K-SEO includes its essential knowledge locally.
