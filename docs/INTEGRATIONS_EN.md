# Connecting sources, tools and outcomes

K-SEO uses its core knowledge to organize work in your environment. This guide
offers ways to connect existing capabilities and develop what a situation
requires. [Italiano](INTEGRATIONS.md).

## Reach what is already available

The receiving assistant understands the website and its activity through
accessible files, repositories, CMS tools, connectors, browsers, APIs, exports
or feeds. It recognizes sources that describe the product, observe its use or
can change its public surfaces. Recent changes, open work and concurrent
contributions matter too: a new offer or updated copy can change priorities.

| Source or tool | Possible contribution |
| --- | --- |
| Repository, CMS and documentation | Content, revisions, drafts, publications and the path for a change |
| Search Console and other provider tools | Queries, pages, indexing and measurements within each report's coverage |
| Site analytics and logs | Observable access sources, paths, events and technical state |
| Catalogue, product documents and owner's sources | Capabilities, offers, conditions, examples, contacts and reasons for their value |
| Relevant feeds, releases and external sources | Changes that may make an update or a new possibility useful |

An adaptation understands access, pagination, fields, time ranges and errors.
An export can be as useful as an API when it provides the knowledge needed.
When a new tool is useful, the receiver acquires its method and develops it
through [evolution](../skills/kseo-evolution/SKILL.md). Access and configuration
belong to the owner's instance.

## Preserve the meaning of data

A collection retains its source, observed object, period, applicable time
zone, unit, coverage and acquisition time. Partial result pages and unavailable
reports remain identifiable. Different tools can cover different windows:
update each measurement where its own collection provides an observation,
preserving subsequent provider corrections.

A zero value comes from a collection that observed that value. An error, an
uncovered interval and an unavailable capability retain their own meanings.
Distinct analytics identities follow the tool's definition; summing them
across days or sources may count the same identity more than once.

A referrer or UTM parameter can indicate the source of a visit. A citation
requires the response itself or a measurement that observes that event.
A recommendation and a completed action carry further information.
[Sources and evidence](../skills/kseo-evidence/SKILL.md) uses these distinctions
to understand consequences.

## Connect facts, questions and opportunities

The receiver can maintain an updatable catalogue of capabilities, conditions,
examples and links, with their sources and revisions. It uses these facts to
understand and write. A changed fact reaches the representations that depend
on it; a small set of existing documents can already serve this purpose.

Read an observed query alongside the associated pages, the intent of the
question and what the activity actually offers. A different page title can
already address that need. New information may call for a better explanation,
a link, a useful function or a distinct page. Content frequency, length and
structure follow this understanding and the useful outcome sought.

For example, a product gains a new capability. The receiver reaches its
source, understands the questions and pages involved, and updates relevant
explanations and examples. The change can serve both a person and the assistant
searching for them. Follow-up observes the owner's chosen outcome: a useful
contact, purchase, download, product use or another observable consequence.

## Care for representations and effects

A source can feed pages, documentation, sitemaps, structured data and material
for other systems. Understand who uses each surface and how it is produced.
When a source changes, update dependent representations through their owners
and read back the published result.

For Google Search, `llms.txt` is neither a requirement nor a visibility factor;
it may serve other consumers that use it. Google removed FAQ rich results on
May 7, 2026. Questions and answers retain their usefulness for readers.
[Current sources](SOURCES.md) support updates when providers change. A successful
IndexNow notification establishes submission; indexing, citation and use
require their respective observations.

Preserve the effect's identity: content or revision, controller, operation and
editorial state. Drafts and publications have their own states. If a request
may have published while the local record is incomplete, reach the external
object and reconcile the result. Controller-supported idempotency keys or
revisions can support safe resumption. A local lock coordinates participating
actors only.

## Keep the work continuing

Within a continuing mandate, the receiver organizes persistence and scheduling
with available capabilities. On reentry it recovers changes, uncertain effects,
concurrent work and observable consequences. Urgency and frequency follow what
can change the outcome, together with available resources.

A consequence updates both site understanding and the method of the competence
that acted. Comparisons retain period, exposure and context changes. An
association informs learning without establishing causation by itself.
Reusable knowledge returns to the owner that needs to acquire, understand or
intervene differently in the next situation.
